Our goal

Enterprise security testing has always been priced for enterprises. We built CyberHive so a five-person startup can get the same rigor — real testers, real reports, real remediation guidance — without the enterprise price tag or the six-week wait. One hive, watching every corner of your site.

What we do

Everything you need to know your risk — nothing you don't.

●

Manual penetration testing

Human testers probe your app the way an attacker would — business logic, auth flaws, chained exploits that scanners miss.

●

Automated vulnerability scanning

Continuous scans across your web app and infrastructure, tuned to cut noise so you're not chasing false positives.

●

Plain-language reports

Every finding comes with severity, real-world impact, and a fix — written for founders and developers, not just security teams.

●

Retesting & sign-off

Ship a fix, we verify it closed the hole. No extra scoping calls, no extra invoice for confirming your work.

How a test runs

One flat scope, from kickoff to fix.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Tell us what's live — marketing site, app, API. We size the engagement to your budget, not the other way around.

  2. 02

    Scan

    Automated scanning maps your attack surface and flags likely weak points within the first day.

  3. 03

    Test

    Our team manually attempts to exploit what the scan surfaces, plus the things scanners always miss.

  4. 04

    Report

    You get a prioritized, plain-language report — what's exposed, how bad it is, and exactly how to fix it.

  5. 05

    Retest

    Push your fixes, we confirm they hold, and you get a clean report to show customers or investors.
